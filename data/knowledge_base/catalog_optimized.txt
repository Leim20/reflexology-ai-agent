Cupping Precautions (拔罐后注意):
- Avoid wind/air-con on treatment day (避免吹风、吹冷气)

- Reduce cold drinks (减少喝冰饮)
- Marks fade in 3-7 days (罐印3-7天会退)
- Avoid intense exercise (避免激烈运动)
- Drink warm water (多喝温水)

Frequency: Once a month, depends on body condition
